Our Story

Making Travel Effortless, One Booking at a Time

Stackly was founded in 2018 by a small team of engineers and travel enthusiasts who were tired of clunky booking sites, hidden fees, and support lines that never picked up. What started as a simple flight-comparison tool quickly grew into a full travel platform covering flights, hotels, and car rentals.

Today our mission remains the same: to make travel effortless for everyone. We negotiate directly with airlines and hotels to secure honest prices, build technology that works seamlessly, and keep real humans on the other end of every support conversation — because your journey deserves nothing less.
